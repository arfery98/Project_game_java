How to run:
  Run Project3_6813112.MainApplication (Java 21 or newer; tested on Java 25 and Java 26).
  No external libraries. There are no image or sound files: every picture and sound effect
  is drawn / generated by the code, so nothing is read from the disk.

What it is:
  You are a little fish in a straw hat who goes fishing. Pick a fishing spot, cast, and catch
  other fish. Sell them, or keep them as bait to catch bigger fish.

How to play (the full guide is in the game: "Help" button or the F1 key):
  1. Cast: click (or SPACE) once, the power bar sweeps, click again. More power = farther cast =
     the lure sinks deeper.
  2. The lure swings in around the tip of your rod. Hold DOWN to slow the swing, hold UP to pull
     the lure up. You only see the water around the lure.
  3. A fish swims up to the lure. The moment it touches the lure it is hooked automatically.
  4. HOLD SPACE or the mouse button to reel. Keep the tension needle in the green zone.
     A bigger fish may attack your catch while you reel it in and bite pieces off it.
  5. Sell the fish (SPACE) or keep it as bait (B).

Rules to remember:
  - Fish have 4 size classes: Small, Medium, Large, Giant. Small and Medium fish live in the upper
    water (down to about 400 cm), Large fish from 400 cm down, Giant fish from 1000 cm.
  - The free purple lure catches Small fish. A bigger fish only bites a smaller fish used as bait
    (keep a Small fish for Medium, a Medium fish for Large, a Large fish for Giant).
  - A better rod catches bigger fish and casts farther. A rod that is too weak for the fish snaps.
  - The shop (Rods, Reels, Lures, Parts) sells gear. Parts fit into the limited slots of a rod and give it skills.
  - 5 fishing spots, 50 kinds of fish (5 legendary fish that only bite the golden lure and need the Legend rod).

Main screens: Title -> Setup (name, spot) -> Game -> Result (leaderboard).
Dialogs: Options, Help, Shop, Fishdex (collection book), Result.
